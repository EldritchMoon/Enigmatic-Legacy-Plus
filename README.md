# Unofficial 26.1.2 Enigmatic Legacy Plus Port

**Original Author:** **[Aizistral](https://aizistral.com/)**

**Current Version:** 1.1.2

**Minecraft Version:** 26.1.2

**NeoForge Version:** 26.1.2.114 or later.

**Dependencies:** CuriosAPI-NeoForge 15.0.0+26.1.2 or later; Patchouli 26.1-94 or later; Caelus API is no longer needed.

**Optional:** JEI.

**Not yet available on 26.1.2:** the AppleSkin, Mystical Agriculture and Thirst integrations are turned off until those mods update.

This mod is designed to bring new magical-themed content to the game, mainly of utilitary nature. New trinkets and powerful relics are restlessly waiting to contribute to your creational and/or destructional purposes, whatever moral is standing behind them, if any at all...

Whatever fate your advent will bring upon this forgotten land.

This is an unofficial 26.1.2 ver porting of Auviotre's **[Enigmatic Legacy+](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus)**, which is based on Aizistral's **[Enigmatic Legacy](https://github.com/Aizistral-Studios/Enigmatic-Legacy)**.
